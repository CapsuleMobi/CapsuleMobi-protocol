# CAPSULE Whitepaper

## Vision

A market should be readable at a glance. CAPSULE compresses the noise of Solana trading into one sealed, legible board that anyone can check in seconds.

## Mission

Give holders and newcomers a calm, honest view of what is moving, where the liquidity sits, and which address is real.

3. **Phase 3, Compare.** Side-by-side token comparison and sortable columns.
4. **Phase 4, Open.** Publish the source and open the feed layer to contributors.
